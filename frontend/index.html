        Backtest — does the BUY signal work?

        Replays the app's BUY score and suggested buy zone, stop and take profit on past daily bars for your watchlist, one day at a time with no look-ahead.

        Uses today's watchlist, so stocks that were delisted or collapsed are missing (survivorship bias) — real results would likely be worse. When the stop and target fall in the same daily bar the stop is assumed to hit first. R = profit ÷ initial risk (entry − stop). Past results don't predict future returns. Not financial advice.
